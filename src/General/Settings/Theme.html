Theme
: Replaces the board's styling as well as <%= meta.name %>'s own interface.
: Picks Dawn or Dark automatically from your OS preference, overriding the choice above.
: Dossier turns each post into a numbered entry in a case file, with stamps, brackets and redaction bars. Classic is the plain card layout.
: DM Mono ships inside the script, so nothing is fetched from a font service.
: Colour used for links, brackets, focus rings and highlights.
: Spacing and padding around posts.
: Full runs edge to edge like the site does. The columns centre the page with margins either side.
: Give opening posts the same panel treatment as replies. In the dossier look this is also where the case file tab and corner brackets go.
: Stamp your own posts and replies to you, and label pinned, closed and archived threads. Dossier look only.
: A faint film of grain over the page. Dossier look only.
: Dim the rest of a thread while the cursor rests on one post.
: Turn off rounded corners.
: Frost the header bar and the settings backdrop. Costs a little performance.
Animations
: Stamp new posts in, ease menus and dialogs open, wipe redaction bars away on hover, and sweep a scanline down the page when the variant flips.
: Lift posts, thumbnails and catalog cards slightly under the cursor.
: The first screen of posts or catalog cards slides in one after another when a page opens.
: By default animations are shortened when your system asks for reduced motion.
Navigation and tools
: A panel at the top of every thread with its number, when it was opened, entries, files, posters and status.
: Show a button in the corner once you have scrolled down.
: Thin bar along the edge of the page showing how far you have scrolled.
: One click inks over every post, name, file and thumbnail on the page until clicked again. The keybind () works either way and can be changed in the Keybinds tab.
